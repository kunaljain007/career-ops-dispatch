# 7-Day Chief of Staff & Strategy Ops Crash Course

To bridge the gap between being a Hard-Tech/Energy Founder and an AI/SaaS Chief of Staff, you must internalize the language of software, data, and SaaS economics. 

This curriculum is designed to take 1-2 hours per day.

---

### Day 1: The Economics of SaaS & Tech Business Models
**Goal:** Understand how software companies make and measure money.
* **Concepts to learn:** 
  * ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue) & MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
  * CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) vs. LTV (Lifetime Value)
  * Net Revenue Retention (NRR) and Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)
  * The rule of 40 (Growth rate + Profit margin >= 40)
* **Resources:** 
  * Read the "SaaS Metrics 2.0" article by David Skok (ForEntrepreneurs).
  * Watch: "SaaS Business Model Explained" by Y Combinator (YouTube).
* **Action:** Look at a public SaaS company's earnings report (like Cloudflare or Snowflake) and locate their NRR and ARR numbers.

### Day 2: Analytics & SQL Fundamentals (Part 1)
**Goal:** Be able to query your own data. A modern CoS shouldn't wait for data engineers.
* **Concepts to learn:** 
  * Relational Databases (Tables, Rows, Columns).
  * Basic Queries: `SELECT`, `FROM`, `WHERE`, `ORDER BY`, `LIMIT`.
* **Resources:**
  * Mode Analytics SQL Tutorial (Basic SQL).
  * SelectStarSQL.com (Interactive).
* **Action:** Complete the first 3 interactive tutorial sections on SelectStarSQL or Mode.

### Day 3: Analytics & SQL Fundamentals (Part 2)
**Goal:** Mastering aggregations and basic joins for strategic reporting.
* **Concepts to learn:**
  * Aggregations: `COUNT`, `SUM`, `AVG`, `GROUP BY`, `HAVING`.
  * Joins: `INNER JOIN`, `LEFT JOIN`.
* **Resources:**
  * Mode Analytics SQL Tutorial (Intermediate SQL).
* **Action:** Write a theoretical query that joins a "Users" table with a "Payments" table to find "Top 10 paying customers."

### Day 4: Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy & RevOps
**Goal:** Understand how an AI/SaaS product goes from engineering to revenue.
* **Concepts to learn:**
  * Sales Lead Growth (SLG) vs. Product Lead Growth (PLG).
  * The Sales Funnel: MQL (Marketing Qualified Lead) -> SQL (Sales Qualified Lead) -> Closed Won.
  * RevOps (Revenue Operations) - aligning marketing, sales, and customer success.
* **Resources:**
  * Read: Wes Kao or Lenny’s Newsletter on GTM Strategies.
  * Research what a modern CRM architecture looks like (Salesforce vs. Hubspot).
* **Action:** Define how your deep-tech B2B sales at HeatBolt map to the standard software MQL/SQL funnel.

### Day 5: Financial Modeling & Runways (The FP&A Crash Course)
**Goal:** Managing the startup budget and runway.
* **Concepts to learn:**
  * 3-Statement Modeling basics (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow).
  * Burn Rate & Runway Calculation.
  * Scenario Planning (Base case vs. Worst case).
* **Resources:**
  * Watch: Stripe’s "Startup Financial Modeling" or similar YouTube overviews.
  * Review open-source startup financial models (e.g., from Taylor Davidson / Foresight).
* **Action:** Build a basic 12-month cash burn spreadsheet in Google Sheets assuming a $5M seed round, $200k/mo burn, and $20k/mo MRR growth.

### Day 6: AI / LLM Ops & Technical Fluency
**Goal:** Since you are applying to AI companies, you must understand the current stack.
* **Concepts to learn:**
  * RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation).
  * Fine-tuning vs. Prompt Engineering.
  * Tokens, Context Windows, and API costs.
* **Resources:**
  * Read: Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) "Emerging Architectures for LLM Applications".
  * OpenAI API documentation (Just the high-level concepts).
* **Action:** Be able to explain the difference between spending $1M on training a custom model vs. spending $1M on an enterprise RAG pipeline.

### Day 7: The "First 90 Days" CoS Framework
**Goal:** Pull it all together into an interview-ready playbook.
* **Concepts to learn:**
  * The OKR framework (Objectives and Key Results).
  * Cadence building (Weekly leadership syncs, Monthly business reviews, Quarterly board prep).
* **Resources:**
  * Read: "Chief of Staff" articles by First Round Review.
  * Read: "Measure What Matters" by John Doerr (Summary).
* **Action:** Write down your personal "First 90 Days" operational plan for the OpenAI or Anthropic CoS role, focusing on auditing data (SQL) and fixing GTM bottlenecks.
