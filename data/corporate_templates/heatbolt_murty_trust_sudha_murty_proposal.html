1. What We Have Built (HeatBolt)

As an IISc Bangalore doctoral researcher in clean energy and someone deeply blessed to have been mentored by the late Shri Ratan Tata, I developed HeatBolt — a completely flameless, zero-electricity, water-activated thermal platform that heats food, beverages, and water to 100°C in minutes without LPG, open fire, or electricity.

2. The Humanitarian Impact for Rural & Underserved India

Key Synergies with Murty Trust & Rural Development Mandates:
  • Child Malnutrition & School Feeding: Providing instantly self-heated, nutritious meals to remote Anganwadis and tribal schools where fuel, firewood, or cooking gas are scarce or dangerous.
  • Women's Health & Indoor Air Pollution: Replacing hazardous indoor biomass cooking smoke, protecting rural mothers and children from respiratory disease.
  • Disaster Relief & Flood Response: Instant hot, hygienic rations and infant nutrition during floods, earthquakes, and emergency evacuations without requiring field kitchens.
  • Extended Shelf-Life: Integrated barrier packaging preserving nutritious ready-to-eat rations for up to 1 year without cold chain or refrigeration.

3. National Validation & Global Momentum

  • Validated by the Indian Armed Forces at Siachen Glacier down to -60°C.
  • Concluded positive high-level discussions with the Prime Minister's Office (PMO), Ministry of Home Affairs, and Ministry of Railways.
  • Ongoing technical evaluations with global food leaders including Nestlé, Barilla, and Mondelēz.
